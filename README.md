### 🐍 Rotating Snakes Perception Study
**R · PsychoPy · Experimental Design · Statistical Analysis**

Investigated how luminance contrast and chromatic saturation influence perceived motion in the Rotating Snakes illusion.

* Includes an R data-analysis workflow to consolidate 36 participant CSV files, clean missing and outlier observations, engineer experimental variables, and reshape behavioral data for statistical analysis
* Analyzed reaction-time and accuracy data using descriptive statistics, repeated-measures ANOVAs, and paired t-tests, and created ggplot2 visualizations to identify and communicate significant differences across experimental conditions
